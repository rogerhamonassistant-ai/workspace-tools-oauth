Roger Workspace Tools · Updated 20 October 2026

Privacy notice

This notice describes a private, owner-operated Google OAuth client for desktop command-line tools.

Access and purpose

Roger Workspace Tools connects Roger Hamon’s Google account to Google Workspace CLI and rclone. Granted permissions allow the tools to read and manage Drive files, including listing, downloading, creating, updating and deleting files when instructed by the owner. Permissions and account selection are shown by Google during authorization.

Where data goes

The tools run on an owner-controlled Mac and communicate with Google APIs. OAuth credentials are kept in the local owner profile, using the tools’ credential storage and operating-system protections. Downloaded files, command output and logs may remain locally until the owner removes them.

The owner may direct selected files or output into other services, backups or AI-assisted workflows. Those transfers depend on the chosen command and provider; this configuration does not promise that every owner-authorized workflow stays on the Mac. It does not independently collect data for advertising, sell Google user data, or use it to train a general-purpose AI model.

This public website receives no OAuth credentials or Drive content. It has no forms, scripts, analytics or tracking cookies. GitHub Pages hosts the static pages and may process normal web-request information under GitHub’s privacy statement.

Owner control

The owner can revoke access through Google Account connections and remove locally stored credentials, files and logs. Revoking access does not delete copies already downloaded or deliberately sent to another service.

Use of Google API data is subject to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements where applicable.

For questions, contact the maintainer. Do not post credentials or private files in public issues.
